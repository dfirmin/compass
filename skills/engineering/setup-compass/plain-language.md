# Plain-language questions

Whenever you ask the user a question that requires a decision (in `/probing`, `/triage`, `/scout`, `/to-questionnaire`, `/improve-codebase-architecture`, or any ad-hoc clarifying question), pair the technical question with a plain-language restatement a non-technical stakeholder could answer on their own.

- Label it **In plain terms**, directly under the technical question, before your recommended answer.
- One or two sentences. No jargon, code identifiers, acronyms, or platform names unless the stakeholder uses them too.
- Say what is being decided and why it matters to the outcome, not how it is implemented.
- The plain line must stand alone: a reader who skips the technical body can still give a usable answer.
- If a question is purely technical with no business consequence, say so in the plain line ("This is an internal implementation choice; no business impact either way") rather than omitting it.

This applies to questions the agent asks, not to its ordinary narration.
